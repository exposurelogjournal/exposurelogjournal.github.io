This is a journal about photography in its many forms. Posts cover landscape, street and travel photography, product and architecture work, practical questions of technique, and the history of the medium. Some entries are short notes, others take a closer look at a single subject or method.

The aim is simple: write clearly about how photographs are made and why they look the way they do. There is no sponsored content here, and no post is written on behalf of a brand or manufacturer.
